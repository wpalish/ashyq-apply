# Provider survey, 2026-10-19 — what each free or self-hosted search option costs and permits

Read on 2026-10-19 by the planner agent from the providers' own pages where they answered, and from third-party
reports where marked; no account was opened and no query was sent. Prices change: ticket 03 reads the real per-query
cost off each dashboard after the replay. "Fit" means the existing `SearchProvider` seam
(`search(query, domains, max_results)`, results as hints, no applicant data).
