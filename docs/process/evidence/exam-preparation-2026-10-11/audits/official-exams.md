# Official exam catalogue for Unimatch preparation

**Access date: 11 October 2026.** Public official exam-owner, university and government sources only. This is a research catalogue and proposed product contract, not an admissions decision, a content licence or a scored evaluation. No paid search vendor, stopped provider/model, university application, source/Git change, copyrighted full-test scrape or closed evaluation input was used.

## 2. Recognition is not a programme requirement

Two verified examples make the distinction concrete:

- **Oxford undergraduate English policy, current page:** TOEFL taken through 20 January 2026 is accepted, but tests from 21 January 2026 are explicitly not accepted while the revised test is reviewed. It also defines component requirements, alternatives and waiver conditions. Do not extrapolate this to Oxford graduate study, every UK university or future intakes. [Oxford admissions policy](https://www.ox.ac.uk/admissions/undergraduate/applying/for-international-students/english-language-requirements-visas).
- **SJTU Chinese-taught international undergraduate 2026:** CSCA subjects are tied to its programme list; separate Chinese and English evidence/alternatives are specified. Regular-round applicants also have an institution-specific entrance exam. Medicine uses independent admissions. The 2026 deadlines are past at access date; this is an adoption example, not permission to reuse dates/requirements for 2027. [SJTU guide](https://global.sjtu.edu.cn/en/study-sjtu/prospective/degree-programs/387), [2026 programme/CSCA subject matrix](https://isc.sjtu.edu.cn/kindeditor/Upload/file/20251124/20251124165651_6651.pdf).

**Visa is a separate decision layer.** UK Student rules distinguish degree-level and below-degree routes and allow eligible higher-education sponsors to assess degree-level English on the CAS. A university accepting DET/ordinary IELTS/TOEFL does not by itself prove a SELT or another country's visa condition is satisfied. [GOV.UK Student English guidance](https://www.gov.uk/student-visa/knowledge-of-english), [Appendix English Language](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/immigration-rules/immigration-rules-appendix-english-language). Do not automatically map a language score or admissions waiver to visa readiness.

| DET | Original dictation, vocabulary/comprehension and brief written/spoken responses; separate language progress from test environment/rules checklist. | [Owner readiness guide/free practice](https://blog.englishtest.duolingo.com/duolingo-english-test-readiness/). Its practice gives an estimated range, not a certificate. |
| HSK | Original level-labelled character, vocabulary, comprehension and production practice; edition-specific syllabus tagging and separate speaking work. | CTI [syllabus/samples/3.0 demo links](https://www.chinesetest.cn/). Do not silently mix legacy and 3.0 levels. |
| CSCA | Original school-level subject drills and academic Chinese terminology mapped to the published selected subject syllabus; no invented national pass mark. | [Owner preparation hub](https://csca.cn/testprep/). Programme/subject confirmation comes before a tailored plan. |

Launch with short diagnostics, authored explanations, review of mistakes and a repeat practice plan. Label their outcomes **practice progress / formative feedback**. No guaranteed score, calibrated official band prediction, admission probability or certificate should be produced from this starter slice. Official-test booking/fees and third-party accounts are separate from Unimatch's included practice.

Use a rights registry per resource: owner, URL, version, access date, permitted action, licence evidence, expiry, attribution. Start external assets as `LINK_ONLY`; in-product content as `ORIGINAL_REVIEWED`. Do not imply exam-owner endorsement or copy logos. A paid subscription and a publicly downloadable test do not establish reproduction rights.

## 5. Extensible country/degree architecture

**Proposed minimum records:**

- `ExamSpec`: stable exam family + edition, provider, role (`language`/`academic`/`subject`), level, test language, delivery mode, effective dates, component/score schema, validity source, official prep URLs and rights status.
- `RequirementEvidence`: institution/programme/campus + intake year, degree and instruction language, applicant category, scholarship/route, accepted exam editions/modes, overall **and** component minima, allowed alternatives, waiver conditions, superscore/retake rules, deadline/expiry reference date, exact official source and checked date. Separate academic/admissions and visa evidence.
- `LearnerEvidence`: official versus self-reported versus practice; exam edition/date/mode, components including unknown/null, verification status and document reference. Never fill missing components, infer a waiver, replace an official test with practice, or convert a concordance table into acceptance.
- `PracticeItem`: original/licensed/link-only provenance, edition/domain/skill, authored answer/explanation, formative feedback, accessibility and review state. No external test-item ingestion by default.

Use explicit states `required`, `optional`, `alternative`, `not_accepted`, `waiver_pending`, `waived_verified`, `unknown`. A general owner-recognition directory can seed discovery but cannot set programme-level readiness. Keep missing/unverified values null. Refresh policy for the target intake and again before booking/application; do not turn a cached 2026 document into 2027 authority.
