**Location:** draft `Questions 1–5`, lines162–170, versus `Draft grading contract`, line182. These are not introduced synonyms or wrong neighbouring objects; their words appear in the original passage. The full keys are good, more specific answers, but the current questions do not justify treating all shorter answers as wrong. The clearest counterexamples are `briefing` and `code`. Thus **the proposed single-literal automatic grader is not accepted as written**. This does not invalidate the headings/features keys or assert a defect in the current application, which has no short-answer grader.

Before integration, either define subject-reviewed per-item accepted variants, or revise stems to require a genuinely unique missing word/phrase. For example, a stem ending in “attend a ___ session” can test `briefing`; one ending in “using a ___ code” can test `download`. A revised draft must keep its own version and receive another item review. Do not silently force all completions to exactly two words or relax validation to arbitrary substrings. Keep wrong objects, extra ungrammatical nouns, over-limit answers and unsupported synonyms rejected. Item2's exact `36` has no observed ambiguity under the stated source-word rule.
