A session is an opaque random token; no applicant profile is placed in the session cookie. An append-only audit trail records identifiers, actions and operation details, which can include error text or payment metadata. It is not a guarantee that every stored error is free of personal data. Logs require access, minimization and retention controls; identifiers are not anonymous data.

The community is different: if you create a community profile, your display name, the city, major and universities you say you are aiming at, the status you choose, your description of yourself, and every post and reply you write are visible to every other signed-in applicant, including people in other workspaces.
