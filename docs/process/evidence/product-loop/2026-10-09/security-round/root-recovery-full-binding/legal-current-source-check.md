- Kazakhstan law, [Adilet](https://old.adilet.zan.kz/rus/docs/Z1300000094): Article12(2) covers storage in a database and/or digital object in Kazakhstan. Article8(7) gives15 workingdays for stopping consent-based processing after withdrawal, or a reasoned refusal where legal retention/processing is required. Article19-1 concerns automated processing that changes rights/interests and includes3 workingdays for objections. This does not establish that every recommendation qualifies, or identify this product's operator/jurisdiction.
