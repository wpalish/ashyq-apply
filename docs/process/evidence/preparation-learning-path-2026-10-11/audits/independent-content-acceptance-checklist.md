## Short-answer positives and negatives

The IELTS v2 contract permits only letter case, surrounding/repeated whitespace and **one optional terminal full stop** to be normalized. Preserve interior words, order, endings and other punctuation. Apply complete equality with the appropriate item's closed accepted forms and the stated maximum of two words and/or a number. Do not add semantic/model/substring grading.

Meaningful positives include `  DIVIDERS.  ` for item1, `36.` for item2, `Condition   SHEET.` and `sheet` for item3, `briefing`/`session` for item4, and `CoDe.` for item5. Each authored accepted form must earn one point; zero correct counts remain possible and are honest.

| Item | Examples that must not earn credit | Reason independent of implementation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | `foam dividers`, `internal frame`, `divider`, `dividers..` | Repeats supplied foam; different part; unaccepted singular; two final periods are outside normalization. |
| 2 | `thirty six`, `36 cards`, `36.0`, `3 6` | Not the source-exact number/form; repeats the supplied noun; decimal form not authored; two separate numbers do not give the stated count. |
| 3 | `inventory`, `paper inventory`, `condition sheet extra`, `sheet condition`, `sheets` | Wrong document; too many words; changed order or unaccepted plural. A phrase merely containing sheet must not pass. |
| 4 | `written test`, `teacher`, `briefings`, `briefing session please` | Test is expressly not required; person is not an event; unaccepted plural; over limit. |
| 5 | `memory stick`, `download code extra`, `codes`, `code!` | Explicitly rejected medium; over limit; unaccepted plural; punctuation not normalized. |

Distinguish a structurally valid wrong answer (raw point0 with post-submission feedback) from malformed transport input (reject without a new receipt/private key disclosure). Cross-item accepted forms must not be credited: e.g. `code` does not answer item3 and `sheet` does not answer item5. Unknown/duplicate/omitted question IDs, wrong answer format, unknown choice IDs, oversized/non-string text and extra unrecognized fields require explicit bounded validation. This is not a reason to reject a legitimate repeated feature choice A.
