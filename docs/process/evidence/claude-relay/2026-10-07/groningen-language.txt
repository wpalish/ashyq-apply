Applying for the February 2027 intake? Please visit 
Bachelor's language requirements (2026/27)
 or 
Master's language requirements (2026/27)

If you are applying as an international student, you will have to prove that you meet the language proficiency requirements for your English-taught programme. To do this, you will have to submit a sufficient language test result to the Admissions Office.

Exemption policy

You may qualify for an exemption from the language test requirement if you completed your secondary or higher education in one of the majority-English speaking countries listed in the exemption list below, or if you passed English as a subject at VWO-level.

You are exempted from the English language test requirement if you can prove your English proficiency through one of the following qualifications:

1. A secondary or higher education diploma from a recognized majority English-speaking country

In order to qualify for this exemption, you must have completed either a
 full (upper) secondary education
, a 
full-time higher education undergraduate degree
 (at least 3 years), or a 
postgraduate degree
 taught 
exclusively in English
 in one of the following recognized majority English-speaking countries:

Antigua and Barbuda, Australia, the Bahamas, Barbados, Belize, Canada (exc. Quebec), Dominica, Grenada, Guyana, Ireland, Jamaica, Malta, New Zealand, Saint Kitts and Nevis, Saint Lucia, Saint Vincent and the Grenadines, Trinidad and Tobago, United Kingdom (inc. British Overseas Territories and Crown Dependencies), United States of America (inc. Guam, Northern Mariana Islands, Virgin Islands)

2. A higher education diploma from an additionally exempted country or region

In order to qualify for this exemption, you must have completed a 
full-time higher education undergraduate degree
 (at least 3 years) or a 
postgraduate degree
 taught 
exclusively in English
, from one of the following countries or regions:

Aruba, Botswana, Cameroon (Anglophone education system only), Curaçao, Eswatini, Fiji, the Gambia, Ghana, Kenya, Lesotho, Liberia, Malawi, Mauritius, Micronesia, Namibia, Nigeria, the Netherlands (inc. Bonaire, Saba, Sint Eustatius, HBO degrees), Papua New Guinea, Puerto Rico (U.S.A.), Quebec (Canada), Rwanda, Seychelles, Sierra Leone, Singapore, Sint Maarten, South Africa, Tanzania, Uganda, Zambia, Zimbabwe

3. A secondary education diploma with English as a subject taught at VWO-equivalent level

In order to qualify for this exemption, you must have obtained a secondary education diploma where English was taught at or equivalent to Dutch VWO-level, and included in the list below:

Validity of certificates

Keep in mind that IELTS, LanguageCert, Pearson PTE, and TOEFL certificates are only valid for 2 years.

Cambridge and Linguaskill certificates do not have limited validity. This means we also accept older CAE (now C1 Advanced) and CPE (Now C2 Proficiency) certificates. For Level 3 programmes, a score of 180+ on the old Linguaskill scale is 
not
 accepted as sufficient proof of profiency.

Online testing options

If you are unable to take a language test in an official test centre due to unsafe conditions or due to test centre closure in your home country, we can accept the following online testing options:

IELTS Online

TOEFL iBT Home Edition

For Iranian applicants affected by the suspension of TOEFL and IELTS testing in Iran, we recommend looking into 
LanguageCert
 testing as a potential alternative. We also accept certificates of tests taken in neighboring countries (Armenia, Azerbaijan, Turkey). If you require further assistance, please contact the Admissions Office through our 
contact form
.

Language level requirements

The University of Groningen uses three English proficiency levels, ranging from level B2 to C1 on the CEFR scale.

Admissions Level 1 (CEFR B2)
 aligns with the national minimum language requirements for higher education, and is the level required for admission to many 
Bachelor's programmes
.

Admissions Level 2 (CEFR B2-C1)
 is the most common proficiency level, and is required for most 
regular Master's programmes
 as well as for many 
Bachelor's programmes
.

Admissions Level 3 (CEFR C1)
 is required for many 
selective 
and 
Research Master's
 programmes.
