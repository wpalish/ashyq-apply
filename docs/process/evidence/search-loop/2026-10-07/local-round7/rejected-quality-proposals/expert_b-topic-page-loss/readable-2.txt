Applying
 Dates & Deadlines
The university application process starts in the fall of the year before you intend to begin your studies. We encourage you to submit your application and your supporting documents well in advance of posted deadlines. 
Admission decisions will be released beginning in January and continue through to late May. Decisions may also be released in the summer, depending on the program and applicant type. If we receive your application from OUAC after the program is closed, your application will be cancelled with no refund.

 Early application consideration
Prospective undergraduate students who wish to be considered for admission in earlier rounds are encouraged to apply by 
November 7
 and submit all required documents by 
December 1
. Our early consideration process works to expedite the review of these applications. Students who apply by November 7 and complete the necessary next steps listed on the 
Join U of T applicant portal
 by December 1 will be eligible to be considered for admission in the first admission round, as well as subsequent admission rounds.
 New to Ontario
If you're currently finishing high school in Ontario but completed Grade 11 outside the province, we encourage you to apply by 
November 7
 and follow the instructions on the 
Join U of T applicant portal
 to submit your Grade 11 results, along with any other required documents, by 
December 1
.
 
 Frequently asked questions
 
 
Expand All
 
 
 What are the English language requirements? 
 
All applicants must demonstrate a high level of English proficiency. If English isn’t your first language, you may need to provide proof through an approved test, unless you qualify for an exemption. 
Learn more about English language requirements.
 
 
 When will I hear from U of T after I apply? 
 
About a week after you apply, you'll get an email from the university with instructions on how to log into your applicant portal. There you'll find your personalized next steps, any required documents, and be able to track your application status.
