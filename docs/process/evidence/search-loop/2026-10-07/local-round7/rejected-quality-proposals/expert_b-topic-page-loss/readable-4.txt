Other dates
September
(Mid September) The online application will be available on the 
Ontario Universities' Application Centre website
. Check with your school/guidance counsellor for details and assistance.
September 26-27: The 
Ontario Universities' Fair
 takes place.
October
(Mid October) Access to the 
Join U of T website
 opens for applicants.
November
November 7: Recommended application deadline for early consideration.
December
(Early December): Document deadline for early consideration. This includes academic reports and transcripts to date, supplementals, and English proficiency test results. 
February
(Varies by year) Deadline to submit an application for the Awards Profile on 
Join U of T
 to be considered for U of T scholarships and awards.
(Varies by year) The first round of admission offers are made to OUAC applicants currently in high school. Keep in mind that only a small percentage of students will receive their offer during this phase.
March
March 1: Deadline for deferred incoming students to confirm return to U of T.
March 31: Deadline to qualify for the residence guarantee. Complete Step 1 of the 
StarRez Application
 soon because after this date, residence may not be guaranteed.
(Varies by year) Monthly round of admission offers are made to OUAC applicants currently in high school.
April
(Varies by year) Monthly round of admission offers are made to OUAC applicants currently in high school.
May
May 1: For Canadian and International applicants, the date by which you must select a university may be as early as May 1; review your offer letter for your specific date
(Late May) The final round of admission offers are made to OUAC applicants currently in high school.
June
June 1: For Ontario high school applicants, the last date by which you must select a university offer to accept on OUAC. Review your offer letter for your specific date.
(Throughout June) We continue to make offers to qualified students on a rolling basis as space remains in programs.
July
(Early July) Deferral request form becomes available on 
Join U of T
 for admitted students wanting to defer their offer to next academic year.
(Throughout July) We continue to make offers to qualified students on a rolling basis as space remains in programs.
August
(Throughout August) We continue to make offers to qualified students on a rolling basis as space remains in programs.
