 Requirements for international applicants
All applicants are required to present a senior/Grade 12 English course for admission consideration. Other prerequisite courses must be presented at the senior/Grade 12 equivalent level. Browse our 
undergraduate programs
 to see if there are additional, program-specific requirements for your intended program of study. 
Conditional offers of admission will be made based on your previous year’s grades and your in-term/mid-term results, if available. You must submit all required documents by the 
published document deadline
 for your program(s) of interest.
If you're a Canadian living or studying outside of Canada, you may have some additonal questions and considerations. 
Find more information on our Canadians living abroad page.

 British-Patterned Education 
 
Minimum admission requirements 
Five different IGCSE/GCSE/Ordinary Level academic subjects 
and
 three different A Level/International A Level academic subjects. Prospective students presenting four AS subjects in lieu of three A Levels will also be considered for most programs.
English IGCSE/GCSE/Ordinary/AS/A Level is required for all programs. Mathematics AS or A Level is required for programs with the Calculus prerequisite. Other prerequisites should be presented at AS or A Level. General Paper, General Studies, Critical Thinking, Thinking Skills, Key Skills, and AS Global Perspectives and Research will not be included in our assessment .
Prospective students who followed a different curriculum prior to their AS/A Levels will be considered on their overall record, provided that they satisfy prerequisites.
Transfer credit may be awarded for your A Level subjects. See the 
Transfer Credit Information
 for further details.
Note about technical qualifications
BTEC National (Level 3), Cambridge Technical (Level 3) and T-Level qualifications are not accepted in lieu of AS or A level subjects.

 Caribbean Advanced Proficiency Examination (CAPE) 
 
Minimum admission requirements
Six CAPE units offered by the Caribbean Examinations Council. English completed at CSEC or CAPE level is required for all programs. Pure Mathematics or Integrated Mathematics at the CAPE level is required for programs with the Calculus prerequisite. Other prerequisite subjects should be presented at the CAPE level.
Transfer credit may be awarded for your CAPE subjects. See the 
Transfer Credit Information
 for further details.

 French Baccalaureate 
 
Minimum admission requirement
Baccalauréat/Baccalauréat Genéral, including Anglais.
Prerequisites
Anglais completed in both the première and terminale years is required for all programs.
Spécialité Mathematics completed in both the première and terminale years will satisfy the Calculus prerequisite. Mathématiques Expertes is also recommended for applicants to programs in Engineering
Spécialité Mathematics (première only) or Math complémentaires will satisfy the Advanced Functions prerequisite.
Spécialité SVT (Sciences de la vie et de la Terre) completed in première only, or, in première + terminale will satisfy the Biology prerequisite.
Spécialité Physique-Chimie completed in première, or, in première + terminale will satisfy the Physics and/or Chemistry prerequisite for admission to arts/science programs. Applicants to programs in Engineering must present Physique-Chimie in premiere and terminale.
Mathématiques Expertes is also recommended in the Terminale year for admission to U of T Engineering programs. Refer to the 
Faculty of Applied Science & Engineering
 for admission requirements for Engineering programs.
Transfer credit may be granted for French Baccalaureate subjects. See the 
Transfer Credit Information
 for details.
 
 
 International Baccalaureate 
 
Minimum admission requirement
International Baccalaureate Diploma, with English HL or SL.
Prerequisites can be presented at the Standard or Higher Level. Be sure to check the requirements for your intended 
area of study
.
The following IB Math courses will satisfy the Calculus and Advanced Functions prerequisites for science and business programs:
Mathematics: Applications and Interpretations HL
Mathematics: Analysis and Approaches SL
Mathematics: Analysis and Approaches HL
Mathematics: Applications and Interpretations SL will satisfy the Advanced Functions prerequisite (only). 
Admission consideration is based on your predicted or final results (excluding bonus points).
If you are completing IB courses but are not enrolled in the full Diploma Programme, you may qualify for admission if you are completing another recognized secondary school diploma. See 
International High School Requirements by Country
 for a list of acceptable qualifications.
The IB Career-related Programme is not accepted as a basis of admission to U of T degree programs.
Transfer credit may be awarded for your IB subjects. See 
Transfer Credit Information
 for more details.
Final IB results must be submitted to U of T electronically by the IBO.

 U.S.-Patterned Education 
 
Minimum admission requirements
High School Diploma from an accredted high school. We expect competitive applicants to be enrolled in a rigorous academic course load, and to have taken the highest level of course available to them in prerequisite subjects.
Find requirements for Engineering 
 
Prerequisite courses
Prerequisite subjects, including English, should be presented at Senior Year/Grade 12 level or AP/IB.
Calculus, AP Calculus (AB or BC) or one semester of college/university calculus is required for most math, science, computer science and commerce programs. Precalculus cannot be used to satisfy the Calculus prerequisite.
Browse prerequisites by program
 
Standardized tests
Students who have written SAT or ACT tests, and wish to have these considered in their assessment, may submit their scores.
Applicants to Engineering are required to fully disclose all test scores.
SAT, ACT and AP scores should be sent to us electronically. SAT and AP institution code is 0982 and ACT institution code is 5366.
Transfer credit may be awarded for your Advanced Placement examinations and/or International Baccalaureate Higher Level subjects. Check out the 
Transfer Credit Information
 for further details.

 Frequently asked questions
 
 
Expand All
 
 
 What documents do I need to submit after I apply? 
 
After you apply, you'll get an email from us with instructions for logging into the Join U of T or Engineering Applicant Portal. That's where you'll find a personalized list of required documents based on your academic background.
 
 
 Can I apply before I finish high school? 
 
Yes. Most students apply in their final year of high school. We may make a conditional offer of admission based on your previous year's grades and your in-term or mid-term results. Offers are conditional on completing your academic year with satisfactory final results, including all required senior-level courses.
 
 
 What are the English language requirements? 
 
All applicants must demonstrate a high level of English proficiency. If English isn’t your first language, you may need to provide proof through an approved test, unless you qualify for an exemption. 
Learn more about English language requirements.
