How to apply to Aalto

Aalto University has an extensive selection of different programmes and multiple student selections. Here you will find application instructions and admission criteria for bachelor's, master's and doctoral programmes.

 Try out the new Applicant’s AI helper 

Application periods for studies starting in 2027

Applying to Master's programmes: 7 December 2026 – 5 January 2027

Applying to Bachelor's programmes in English: 7 – 22 January 2027

Applying to Bachelor's programmes in Finnish/Swedish (National joint application, spring 2026): 9 – 23 March 2027
