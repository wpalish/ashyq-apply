 About us

 
 Aalto WiT Programme is led by a team of researchers, educators and field workers from Aalto WiTLAB, all equipped with a unique set of expertise and experience. Find out more about us and our expertise on this page!
 
 

Our expertise in layers

Our expert and experienced faculty members from different fields apply the new pedagogical methods in a transformative learning environment to build and bridge capacities needed to work in the fields of humanitarian architecture, emergency response and sustainable development. The nexus of expertise and approaches our faculty members bring to the WiT Programme can be seen from the illustration below.
