Study at Aalto

 Study computer science

 
 Explore the diverse world of computer science at Aalto University, ranked among the top 100 globally. Dive into software engineering, cyber security, and telecommunication engineering, or specialise in artificial intelligence (AI) and machine learning. For those drawn to creativity and innovation, studies in human-computer interaction, game design, and sound engineering provide a human-centric approach to technology. Choose either a master’s degree programme or opt for separate courses from our Lifewide learning portfolio.
 
 

Aalto's computer science studies emphasise innovation, research, and practical skills in key areas such as cyber security, software engineering, machine learning, AI, and ICT. They combine theory and practical knowledge, preparing you for a career in industry or academia, or boosting your current career path. Whether you want to focus on programming or apply computer science methods to fields like health technology engineering, Aalto’s courses offer the ideal environment to shape your future.

Why study computer science at Aalto?

Among the top in the world.
 Aalto ranks #88 in Computer Science (THE), #51-75 in telecommunication (QS), and #20 in Human-Computer Interaction (CSRankings).

Unique fields.
 Aalto is among the top 25 universities for multidisciplinary collaboration. We teach engineering and technology, business and economics, as well as art and design. Students can customise their degrees by combining studies from all fields.

Practical and flexible. 
You have access to world-class facilities, industry partnerships, and a vibrant, international community. You can pick individual courses or choose full-time degree studies.

Completing a master’ degree at Aalto is designed to take two years and it includes studies on campus. Lifewide learning studies are organised either online or on campus depending on the course or module.

Human-centric and creative technologies 

Bridge the gap between technology and user experience. Learn how to design intuitive interfaces, create immersive digital experiences, and develop technologies that make a difference in people's lives. These studies focus on the intersection of computer science and human interaction, offering opportunities for creative problem solvers to work on areas like human-computer interaction (HCI), acoustics, and game design. You'll get to learn from highly appreciated academics – Aalto University’s HCI research ranks in the top 20 globally (CSRankings).
