 Apply to Bachelor's programmes in English

 
 At Aalto University, you can study in bachelor’s programmes in English in the fields of business and economics, science and technology as well as art and design. The next application period for programmes taught in English is 7–22 January 2027. On this website, you'll find the descriptions and application guidelines for all Aalto's bachelor's programmes in English. You will also find information on student admissions and experiences of studying at Aalto!

 Are you already a student at Aalto University or an Aalto University graduate?
 

As an Aalto University student, you cannot be granted a right to pursue a degree in a study option if you have already earned a degree or have a valid right to pursue one in that study option. 

If you have a valid right to study only for a Master of Science (Economics and Business Administration) at the Aalto University School of Business, you cannot be admitted to the undergraduate level study options in the field of business and economics at Aalto University.

 One study right rule
 

If you have a study right at a Finnish higher education institution, please note the amendment to the Universities Act and the new one study right rule.

Under the amended Universities Act (42 a §), your study rights at the same or lower level will be terminated if you accept a new study right leading to a degree.

Previous study rights remain valid until the new study right begins and are terminated on the preceding day. The study right granted in Aalto University’s bachelor's admissions begins on 1 August.

This rule applies only to study rights at higher education institutions in Finland.

Read more about the 
one study right rule on Studyinfo
 (studyinfo.fi). 

When to apply?

You apply either in Admission round A or Admission round B. The application period for both rounds is from 7 to 22 January 2027. Read the section 
Application process
 on this page to find which admission round is correct for you.

Who can apply?

All applicants must fulfill the general eligibility requirements for higher education and submit the required documents by the given deadlines. 

You are eligible to apply if you have completed or will complete during spring 2027 one of the following qualifications:

Finnish matriculation examination

International Baccalaureate (IB) diploma, completed in an accredited IB school

European Baccalaureate (EB) diploma, completed in an accredited EB school

Deutsches Internationales Abitur diploma (DIA) or Reifeprüfung diploma (RP), completed in Finland

Upper secondary education completed outside Finland, for example a high school diploma. The qualification must give eligibility to the corresponding level of higher education in the country in which it was awarded.

A Finnish vocational upper secondary qualification, further vocational qualification or specialist vocational qualification as set in the Vocational Education Act (531/2017)

Advanced International Certificate of Education diploma (AICE)

Additionally eligible to apply are

applicants who have been granted the right to study in a university in Finland

applicants who have a higher education degree completed in Finland

applicants who have at minimum a bachelor level degree completed outside Finland

 Application process

 
You apply either in 
Admission round A or Admission round B
. The application period for both rounds is from 7 to 22 January 2027, but the admission results are published at different times. The correct admission round depends on the study option (or options) you are applying to and your educational background. Check the information below to find the correct application round.

 

 Applying to the Bachelor’s programme in Design and Media
 

The only scenario in which you can apply in both application rounds is if you are applying to the fields of science and technology or business and economics on the basis of SAT or ACT test results as well as to the Design and Media programme, and your eligibility is based on a qualification other than the Finnish matriculation examination, International Baccalaureate (IB), European Baccalaureate (EB), or a Finnish Reifeprüfung (RP) or Deutsche Internationale Abitur (DIA). In this case, you must submit a separate application form and required application documents for each round. Design and Media has 
its own instructions page
.

You can apply to a maximum of three study options within one admission round. You may only be admitted to one study option. View all study options available at Aalto University on the page 
Study options
.

Read the application instructions and document requirements carefully. You can edit your application form during the application period. Upload all the required application documents to your application form by the given deadlines.

If you want to apply in any other application rounds as well, you must separately complete the correct application form during the application period and submit any required attachments by the given deadlines. If you apply in multiple application rounds, submitting the attachments for one application is not sufficient; you must submit them separately for each application round.

Order of preference of study options

Rank your study options in the order of preference on the application form. Place highest the study option you are most interested in. Keep in mind that if you are admitted to your first or second preference option, your lower preferences will be automatically cancelled. You can change the study options or their order of preference only during the application period, from 7 to 22 January 2027. 

 

 Application fee
 

Applicants who are not citizens of the European Union (EU), or the European Economic Area (EEA) member states, or Switzerland must pay a €100 application fee to apply for higher education in Finland. 

Citizens of EU or EEA member states and Switzerland are exempt from the fee. In addition, certain types of Finnish residence permits provide an exemption from the fee. Detailed information on the application fee, exemptions and payment instructions are available on the Studyinfo page 
Application fee
.

If you have a document that exempts you from the application fee, you must upload it to your application form.

The application fee is collected by the Finnish National Agency for Education in connection with filling out the application form in the Studyinfo.fi service. Payment must be made within seven days of submitting your application. The fee cannot be paid late. Unpaid applications will not be processed.

The application fee is statutory and non-negotiable; no waivers are available.

Please note that paying the fee does not guarantee admission to university. 

If you have questions about application fees after reviewing the 
Application fee
 page, please contact the National Agency for Education at applicationfee@oph.fi.

 Accepting your study place offer
 

If you have been admitted to a study programme, you will receive an email notification. You can only be admitted to your highest study option preference for which your admission results are sufficient. 

When all the admission results have been published, you can be admitted off the waiting list. Please note that if all study places have been filled, no one will be admitted off the waiting list. Admission from the waiting list ends on 3 August 2027, or earlier if all study places are filled.

You must accept the offer of admission by the given deadline, otherwise the admission offer will be cancelled. The offer acceptance is binding. Once you have accepted the offer, the acceptance cannot be cancelled. When you bindingly accept the admission offer, your applications to other study options will be cancelled automatically.

View more information about accepting your study place and other information for newly admitted students on the 
new bachelor’s students
 page. The page will be updated in spring 2027.

Important information about accepting your study place offer and enrolment can be found at 
Studyinfo
.

 You can accept only one study place leading to a higher education degree in Finland
 

You can only accept one study place leading to a higher education degree in Finland for education that begins in the same academic term. 
The one study place per term provision
 applies to all higher education in universities and universities of applied sciences in Finland. 

If you applied to multiple study options or in different application rounds, it is likely that you will not receive the admission results for all of your study option preferences at the same time. Think carefully whether you wish to accept an offered study place or wait for the results of your other study options.

 Submitting a rectification request
 

The rectification request procedure gives you the possibility to appeal your admission results if you believe that there has been an error in the admission decision-making process. The rectification request must be submitted to the University Registry within 14 days of the publication of the admission results. The University will not process rectification requests received after the deadline or before the publication of the results. Rectification request must contain the grounds for your request. Rectification requests are not intended to be used as a tool for requesting feedback or additional information on the admission process, including admission results.

For details, see 
instructions for requesting rectification to student admissions
.
