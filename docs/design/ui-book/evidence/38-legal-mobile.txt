				146 text What is stored.
				147 text  The applicant profile you fill in (education, grades, test scores, citizenship, country of residence, budget and preferences), the research runs you start, their results and your decisions and notes on them, and the evidence behind every value: the address of each official page that was read, the excerpt taken from it and the date it was read.
				148 text Accounts.
				149 text  When authentication is enabled, an account holds an email address, a display name and a password that is stored only as a scrypt hash. A session is an opaque random token; no applicant profile is placed in the session cookie. The browser also stores local drafts, case pointers, theme preferences and document ticks; those are not encrypted storage. An append-only audit trail records identifiers, actions and operation details, which can include error text or payment metadata. It is not a guarantee that every stored error is free of personal data. Logs require access, minimization and retention controls; identifiers are not anonymous data.
				150 text The community is public, and it is the only part that is.
				151 text The applicant case is shared inside its workspace rather than public. The community is different: if you create a community profile, your display name, the city, major and universities you say you are aiming at, the status you choose, your description of yourself, and every post and reply you write are visible to every other signed-in applicant, including people in other workspaces. That is the point of it — but it is a separate decision, and registering an account does not make it for you. Nothing from your applicant case, your research or your shortlist appears there. You can leave at any time, which deletes your community profile, your posts and your replies and keeps your account and your research.
				152 text A picture, if you add one.
				153 text  It is stored here rather than anywhere else, and served only to signed-in applicants. Before it is stored, the metadata is stripped — a photograph taken on a phone records where it was taken, and that is not something to publish alongside a school leaver's name. Nothing is inferred from the image: it is not scanned, matched against anything, or used to identify you.
				154 text Private messages.
				155 text  Conversations are stored so both people can read them, and are visible to nobody else through ordinary conversation endpoints — with one exception, which exists so that harassment can be dealt with: either person in a conversation can report a message, and reporting it shows that message to a moderator. Who may write to you first is your setting, and it starts narrow. Messages are not end-to-end encrypted: authorized operators with database access can technically access stored content. Leaving the community is not the same as deleting the account or a conversation.
				156 text What leaves the service.
				157 text  University-page reads and configured search-provider queries use privacy-validated programme, degree, intake and public audience context, which may derive from your preferences. The normal query contract excludes the full profile, identity, contact details, grades and budget; it is not a promise that every query is anonymous. The fetcher honours robots.txt, reads nothing behind a login and bypasses no CAPTCHA. If password-reset email is enabled, your email address and reset message reach the configured mail provider. If you choose a payment, the payment provider receives the information required for that transaction; a masked phone in our database does not mean the provider received only a masked phone. Hosting and network operators can process connection metadata. Their actual identities, countries and retention terms must be disclosed by this deployment. No advertising or analytics SDK is present in the reviewed application source; that is not a verification of every deployed infrastructure log.
				158 text What you can do with it.
				159 text  Export the case record the API makes available as JSON, and delete a case or your account. The case export is not an export of every account, payment, community or operational record. Account deletion erases workspaces where you are the only member; a shared workspace and its cases remain for its other members. A deletion error must not be reported as success. Security audit records, operational job records, recipients’ copies and backups require their own retention and deletion review; deleting a live case is not proof that every copy everywhere disappeared.
				160 text How long it is kept.
				161 text  The current application has deletion controls but no universal automatic expiry for every data class. The operator must publish a purpose-based retention schedule for profiles, account records, payments, audit/job records and backups, and demonstrate that it is applied. Backup settings and a planned deletion procedure are not evidence that a backup was actually erased. Exports you download remain on your device until you remove them.
				162 text Applicants under 18.
				163 text  The product is built for people applying to undergraduate study, so it will be used by minors. It does not currently ask for age, verify it, or ask for a parent's consent. That is a gap a legal review has to close, not a decision that this is acceptable.

				168 text What this service is.
				169 text  It reads published admission and funding criteria and shows you how your profile sits against them, with a source and a date behind every value. It is a research assistant, not an adviser and not an agent.
				170 text What it never does.
				171 text  It never predicts whether you will be admitted or funded, and no number on any screen is a probability of either. It never submits an application, signs anything, or pays anything on your behalf. It never fills a gap in the evidence with a guess: a value that could not be confirmed is shown as unknown, and two sources that disagree are both shown to you.

				176 text Acceptable use.
				177 text  Do not use the service to harvest data about other people, to evade a site's stated crawling rules, or to submit applications automatically. Reading the community and its profiles is what they are there for; copying them out in bulk, or contacting people through anything you learn there without being asked, is not. The fetcher enforces the crawling rules; automatic submission it simply cannot do.
				178 text What you write in the community.
				179 text  You keep what you write and stay responsible for it. Do not post another person's private information, impersonate anyone, or harass, and do not present a rumour about a university's requirements as a fact — the rest of the product exists precisely because published criteria and hearsay are not the same thing. The operator of a deployment may remove a post or an account. There is a reporting button on posts, answers, messages and profiles, and a queue that a moderator named by this deployment works from. There is no automated filtering and no alert when a report arrives, so how soon anything happens depends on a person opening the queue. If you need something to stop now rather than soon, blocking is the tool that works without waiting for anybody.
