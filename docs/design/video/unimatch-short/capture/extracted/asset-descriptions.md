# Assets (no-capture path — built from the product's own tokens and data)

- capture/assets/images/globe-night.svg — Unimatch's night globe: a dark disc (#101014 → #22222C)
  with ~1,350 visible land dots in three depth greys, centred over Central Asia (2000×2000 viewBox).
  Same land data and density as the app's landing story. Use as the hero plate of the routes beat.
- capture/assets/images/routes.json — six great-circle routes from Astana on that globe's viewBox
  (Groningen, Leuven, Vienna, Tokyo, Singapore, Toronto), each with its pass slot (country colour),
  the SVG path `d`, and its end point; `home` is Astana's point. Draw them as SVG strokes.
- capture/assets/fonts/inter-latin-{400,500,600,700,800}-normal.woff2 — Inter, the brand face.
- capture/assets/images/unimatch-logo.svg — the owner's logo: the lowercase wordmark "unimatch"
  whose m is two interlocking strokes; traced from the supplied artwork, one colour (currentColor),
  viewBox 3700×736. Use it in ink on paper, in cream on night.
- No screenshots: the product's components (passes, the money sheet, answer chips, tickets) are
  rebuilt in HTML from the design system — they are the product's own UI, not stock imagery.
