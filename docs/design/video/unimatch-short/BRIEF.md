## Intent

Repurpose a 30-second SaaS promo (source: a ClimbX launch video — dark cold open, a nagging
question, logo, feature beats with big kinetic headlines, a dark emotional beat, a proof graph,
an end card with stats and a trial button) into a fresh vertical short for Unimatch. Keep the core
idea — a nagging question answered by the product in fast, confident beats — and the pacing
(a new beat every 2–4 seconds, ~30 s). Make the visuals, script and structure original: Unimatch's
own night globe, passes in country colours, the money line by line and the three separate answers,
in palette E («Чернила») and Inter.

## Customizations

- Unnarrated; an original synthesized music bed (no library or source audio), cuts on its bars.
- Every figure is the demo run's and is labelled as an example from demo data.

## Notes

- Product invariants: never a chance, probability or percentage of admission; requirements,
  profile and money stay three separate answers; "if awarded" stays next to a grant-reduced price;
  a fact carries its source and date; nothing invented (no fake user counts, reviews or URL).
- No public URL exists yet (the owner has not approved a deploy): no-capture path, the brand from
  the product's own tokens (frontend/src/styles/tokens.css).
- Interface language is English for now.
