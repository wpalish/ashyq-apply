# Reference research —22October2026

The owner asked for Apple as the base and inspiration from the Eleken collection. This was a bounded review of that50-example collection and relevant primary sites, not a claim to have reviewed the entire internet.

## Observations and application

| Reference | Observed evidence | Unimatch interpretation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Apple homepage](https://www.apple.com/) | Native browser opening shows a quiet compact navigation, centred product headline, concise supporting text, two action levels and a large product image. | Public hero: a single message, primary search action and original globe composition. No Apple assets or marketing copy copied. |
| [Apple iPhone](https://www.apple.com/iphone/) / [current product](https://www.apple.com/iphone-18-pro/) | Text extraction confirms separate highlights, product chapters and comparison. Exact motion curves/mobile pixels were not measured. | Layered explanation from profile to options, source review and plan; existing programme local tabs remain separate from application navigation. |
| [Apple layout guidance](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/layout) | Group related content; adapt hierarchy and navigation to the use scene. | Operational pages keep task focus and stable editing. More space between groups; readable type and dense data remain distinct. |
| [Apple materials](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/materials) | Materials distinguish controls/layers from content. | Translucent navigation only. Source quotes, forms, amounts and requirements remain opaque. |
| [Eleken Adidas Arena example](https://www.eleken.co/blog-posts/best-website-design-examples#35-adidas-arena) / [primary site](https://www.adidasarena.com/) | Eleken image inspected in native browser: oversized typography and layered editorial/architectural composition. Primary text confirms an event-focused path. | Large typography and two tilted explanatory hero layers, restricted to marketing. No sports identity, rotated tables or perpetual movement. |
| [Pitch](https://pitch.com/) | Primary content separates creation, collaboration and delivery; Eleken describes product-led storytelling. | Four-step native-button tour lets visitors explore the relationship between sections. Clearly labelled as a guide, never actual results. |
| [Stripe Press](https://press.stripe.com/) | Primary material is organised around individual books and returning to the shelf. Animation claims come from Eleken and were not replayed. | Keep programme identity and clear back navigation. No WebGL catalogue or simulated rotating documents. |
| [UNITED24](https://u24.gov.ua/uk/rebuild/map) | Collection uses project overview/details to explain transparency. | Evidence states and source applicability remain explicit; do not invent live statistics or scores. |

## Decisions

Apple sets the visual system. The other references contribute individual ideas within it. The Unimatch orbit, blue actions, evidence meanings and original application behaviours remain. The result is not a literal Apple clone and makes no assertion of measured pixel fidelity or real-device frame rate.

Read-only reference research was delegated to two helpers. A third read-only source reviewer found two small issues: labelled tour updates and conditional storage wording. Both were addressed by the sole code writer. No helper changed files or operated production.
