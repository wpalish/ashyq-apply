## Why each beat is honest

- **Beat 2:** the Talent Grant's coverage table lists tuition, fees, housing and meals. The cost
  table adds insurance (€1 450) and books (€700), and the grant does not list them. This is the
  table, not a claim about «всё».
- **Beat 3:** 16 500 + 600 + 7 200 + 3 600 + 1 450 + 700 = 30 050 in costs, minus the €28 350
  grant, leaves €1 700. At $1 = €0.92 that is $1 848. These are the same figures as the first
  video.
- **Beat 4:** the demo run returned 20 programmes in 15 countries, and the render asserts both.
- **Beat 6:** Vienna is «not computable» in the run, and the concept board gives the reason.
