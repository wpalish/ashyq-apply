International Major Entrance Scholarship

Renewable entrance award for outstanding international students.

Value

The award is worth CAD 40,000 per year for 2026/27.

What the award covers

CostStatus
TuitionCovered
Mandatory feesPartially covered

Eligibility

International students of any nationality are eligible to apply.

How to apply

No separate application is required. All admitted students are considered automatically.

Duration and renewal

The award is renewable for up to 4 years of study. Maintain an 80% average and full-time registration.

This award may not be combined with other university scholarships.
