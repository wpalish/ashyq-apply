Melbourne International Undergraduate Scholarship

A fee remission applied to the first year of study.

Value

The award is worth AUD 10,000 per year for 2026/27.

What the award covers

CostStatus
TuitionPartially covered

Eligibility

International students of any nationality are eligible to apply.

How to apply

No separate application is required. All admitted students are considered automatically.

The scholarship deadline is 15 January 2026. This is earlier than the admission deadline.

Duration and renewal

This is a one-time award and is not renewable.

This award may not be combined with other university scholarships.
