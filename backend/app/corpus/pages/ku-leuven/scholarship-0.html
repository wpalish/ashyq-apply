Flemish Community Tuition Grant

Open to students holding EEA or Swiss nationality. Applicants from outside the EEA are not eligible.

Value

The award is worth EUR 6,800 per year for 2026/27.

What the award covers

CostStatus
TuitionCovered

Eligibility

This award is open only to citizens of European Economic Area, Switzerland. Applicants of other nationalities are not eligible.

How to apply

A separate scholarship application must be submitted in addition to the admission application.

Duration and renewal

This is a one-time award and is not renewable.

This award may not be combined with other university scholarships.
