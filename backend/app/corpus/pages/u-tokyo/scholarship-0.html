MEXT Undergraduate Scholarship (University Recommendation)

Covers tuition and fees in full and provides a monthly stipend plus return airfare. Housing is not provided.

Value

The award is worth JPY 1,596,000 per year for 2026/27.

What the award covers

CostStatus
TuitionCovered
Mandatory feesCovered
Living stipendCovered
TravelCovered

Eligibility

International students of any nationality are eligible to apply.

How to apply

Candidates are nominated by the department. Direct applications are not accepted.

The scholarship deadline is 1 November 2026. This is earlier than the admission deadline.

Duration and renewal

The award is renewable for up to 4 years of study. Maintain satisfactory academic progress each year.

This award may not be combined with other university scholarships.
