Justus & Louise van Effen Excellence Scholarship

Covers tuition, mandatory fees, accommodation and a monthly living stipend.

Value

The award is worth EUR 30,000 per year for 2026/27.

What the award covers

CostStatus
TuitionCovered
Mandatory feesCovered

Eligibility

International students of any nationality are eligible to apply.

How to apply

Candidates are nominated by the department. Direct applications are not accepted.

The scholarship deadline is 1 December 2026. This is earlier than the admission deadline.

Duration and renewal

The award is renewable for up to 3 years of study. Remain in the top 10% of the cohort.

This award may not be combined with other university scholarships.

30 awards are offered each year.
