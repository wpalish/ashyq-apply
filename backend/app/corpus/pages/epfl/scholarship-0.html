EPFL Excellence Fellowship

Awarded on the nomination of the section; no direct application is accepted.

Value

The award is worth CHF 16,000 per year for 2026/27.

What the award covers

CostStatus
TuitionCovered
Living stipendPartially covered

Eligibility

International students of any nationality are eligible to apply.

How to apply

Candidates are nominated by the department. Direct applications are not accepted.

The scholarship deadline is 15 December 2026. This is earlier than the admission deadline.

Duration and renewal

The award is renewable for up to 2 years of study. Renewal conditions apply.

This award may not be combined with other university scholarships.
