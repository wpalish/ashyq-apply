BSc Computer Science

EPFL, Lausanne, Switzerland. Entry for the fall 2027 intake.

Entry requirements

Applicants must have studied mathematics, physics at upper secondary level.

English language requirements

Applicants must hold an IELTS Academic certificate with an overall band of 7.0, with no individual component below 6.5. IELTS General Training is not accepted. Certificates must have been issued within the last two years.

TOEFL iBT: a minimum total score of 100 is accepted as an alternative.

Additional requirements

  • An entrance examination is required for all applicants.

Key dates

The application deadline for the fall 2027 intake is 15 January 2027 at 23:59 CET. Late applications are not considered.

Documents

  • Certified copy of the secondary school diploma and full transcript (PDF, max 10 MB)
  • Official English translation of any document not issued in English
  • Copy of the identity page of your passport
  • Personal statement, maximum 650 words
  • One academic reference, submitted directly by the referee
  • Curriculum vitae, maximum 2 pages

An application fee of USD 100 applies. Fee waivers are available on request for applicants demonstrating financial hardship.

Careers

Semester-long industry internship required before graduation.
