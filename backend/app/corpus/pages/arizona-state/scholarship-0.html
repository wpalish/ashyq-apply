New American University Scholarship - Provost Award

Our most generous international award - a full ride to an ASU degree. The award covers non-resident tuition and mandatory fees. Housing and meal plans are the student's responsibility.

Value

The award is worth $36,600 per year for 2026/27.

What the award covers

CostStatus
TuitionCovered
Mandatory feesCovered
HousingNot covered
Meal planNot covered

Eligibility

International students of any nationality are eligible to apply.

How to apply

No separate application is required. All admitted students are considered automatically.

Duration and renewal

The award is renewable for up to 4 years of study. Maintain a 3.0 cumulative GPA and full-time enrolment.

This award may be held together with other university scholarships.
