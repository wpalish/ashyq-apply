Groningen Talent Grant

Covers tuition, all mandatory university fees, university housing and a meal allowance.

Value

The award is worth EUR 28,350 per year for 2026/27.

What the award covers

CostStatus
TuitionCovered
Mandatory feesCovered
HousingCovered
Meal planCovered

Eligibility

International students of any nationality are eligible to apply.

Applicants must have an IELTS score of at least 6.5.

How to apply

A separate scholarship application must be submitted in addition to the admission application.

The scholarship application requires two additional essays: a statement of motivation (maximum 800 words) and a description of your leadership experience (maximum 500 words).

The scholarship deadline is 1 February 2027. This is earlier than the admission deadline.

Duration and renewal

The award is renewable for up to 3 years of study. Maintain a 3.3 GPA and 60 ECTS per year.

This award may not be combined with other university scholarships.
