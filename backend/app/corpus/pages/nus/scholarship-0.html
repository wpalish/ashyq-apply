NUS Financial Aid for International Students

The quantum of assistance depends on an assessment of demonstrated financial need.

Value

The value of the award is determined individually.

What the award covers

CostStatus
TuitionPartially covered

Eligibility

International students of any nationality are eligible to apply.

How to apply

A separate scholarship application must be submitted in addition to the admission application.

The scholarship deadline is 31 March 2027. This is earlier than the admission deadline.

Duration and renewal

This is a one-time award and is not renewable.
