Lester B. Pearson International Scholarship

Covers tuition, books, incidental fees and full residence support for four years.

Value

The award is worth CAD 89,000 per year for 2024/25.

What the award covers

CostStatus
TuitionCovered
Mandatory feesCovered
HousingCovered
Meal planCovered

Eligibility

International students of any nationality are eligible to apply.

How to apply

Candidates are nominated by the department. Direct applications are not accepted.

The scholarship deadline is 15 November 2026. This is earlier than the admission deadline.

Duration and renewal

The award is renewable for up to 4 years of study. Maintain satisfactory academic standing each year.

This award may not be combined with other university scholarships.
